Root Cause Analysis of Manufacturing Quality Deviations Using Process Data: Develop a data-driven system that ingests multivariate process data from a production line (public datasets such as the Bosch production line or SECOM dataset) to identify the process parameters and stages most responsible for quality defects. The solution should handle missing and imbalanced data, provide explainable root-cause rankings, suggest corrective parameter changes, and estimate scrap reduction and cost savings. Software only; no hardware required.
